# Two Best Friends

*A short love story. No gameplay, commands or SQLite tables were harmed.*

They met on a Friday night in a half-empty server, both holding the same
rusty pistol and both too stubborn to give it up. By the end of the round
they were splitting the loot and arguing about who had really made the kill.

After that they were inseparable. They queued together, died together and
blamed each other for it every single time. They knew each other's habits:
one always took the high ground, the other always forgot to reload.

For years they told everyone they were "just best friends."

Then one rainy evening the server crashed mid-round, and in the silence
before the reconnect, one of them typed into the chat:

> "You know, I don't actually mind losing as long as it's with you."

The reply came a long minute later.

> "Took you long enough."

They were married the following spring. The ceremony was held in the lobby,
the guest list was every regular on the server, and the best man had to be
told twice to stop shooting during the vows. The rings were crafted, not
looted, and neither one would admit to who had cried first.

They still queue together every Friday night. They still argue about the
rusty pistol. Neither of them has ever won that argument, and they have
stopped caring.

*The end.*
